Privacy policy

What the site collects, and why.

This notice separates hosting data, optional site analytics, and the personal details you choose to submit through the contact form.

Effective and last updated: 29 July 2026 · Policy version 2026-07-29

01 · Scope

Who this notice covers.

This policy applies to the Docs Forge project website at adhit-r.github.io/docs-forge. Docs Forge is maintained by the repository owner adhit-r.

For a private data request, use a non-public contact method listed on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. Do not place an email address, phone number, or other personal information in a public GitHub issue.

02 · Collection

Three separate data paths.

GitHub Pages hosting

GitHub hosts this static website. GitHub states that it logs and stores a visitor’s IP address for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited. GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement.

Cookie and analytics preferences

The site stores your necessary and analytics choice in your browser’s local storage. A small local receipt history records the purpose, choice, time, collection point, and policy version. This is used to remember and demonstrate your choice on that device.

PostHog analytics is optional. It is not loaded until you grant analytics consent and a PostHog project key has been configured. The integration disables autocapture and session recording. It measures page views and a successful form-submission event without sending the form’s email, phone, or message to PostHog.

Contact form

If contact collection is enabled, the form collects your email address, phone number, and message so the maintainer can respond. Consent to use those details for the response is required. Consent for occasional product updates is separate, optional, and unticked by default.

GitHub Pages cannot process a form itself. Until a secure form endpoint is named in the site configuration, the submit button remains disabled and no contact details are sent. Before enabling collection, this notice must be updated with the processor’s name, processing location, and retention terms.

03 · Purposes

One choice per purpose.

Strictly necessary storage Remember your privacy choice and maintain the consent receipt on your device.
Site analytics Understand aggregate page use through PostHog, only after opt-in.
Respond to an enquiry Use the email, phone, and message you submit to answer your request.
Product updates Send occasional Docs Forge updates only when you separately opt in.

04 · Your choices

Change or withdraw consent.

You can reject analytics, grant it later, or withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing analytics stops PostHog capture and clears its local identity and persistence where the SDK is active. It does not affect the strictly necessary preference record.

For contact details already submitted, request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal through a private contact method on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. If you withdraw product-update consent, the details may still be retained where needed to complete an active enquiry or meet applicable obligations.

05 · Retention and sharing

Limited to the stated job.

Browser consent preferences remain on the device until you clear site data or replace your choice. Contact retention must be configured and disclosed with the selected form processor before collection is enabled.

Data is not sold. It may be handled by GitHub for hosting, by PostHog after analytics consent, and by the configured contact-form processor after that service is named and enabled. These providers may process data in countries other than your own under their terms and safeguards.

06 · Security and updates

Keep the notice aligned with reality.

The site uses HTTPS. No website or transmission method is risk-free, so submit only the information needed for your enquiry.

This notice will be updated when a contact processor, consent manager, analytics region, retention period, or material data practice changes. The effective date and policy version at the top identify the notice linked to each consent receipt.
